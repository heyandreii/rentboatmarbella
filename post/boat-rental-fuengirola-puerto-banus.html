Boat rental in Fuengirola: the private alternative to the shared boats

Updated August 2026 · 6 min read

Search for boat rental in Fuengirola and you will nearly always land on the same thing: the excursion boats leaving the seafront and the marina. Individual tickets, a fixed timetable, forty people aboard and a route already decided before you step on. Nothing wrong with that for what it is — but it is not a private charter.

This guide is about the other option: the whole boat for your group, with a home port in Puerto Banús, about thirty minutes from Fuengirola.

A shared boat and the whole boat are not the same product

The difference is not the price per head, it is the plan. On a shared trip the boat does not stop where you want, does not anchor when the water looks good, and does not turn back when the group has had enough. On a private charter, it does.

  • Up to 10 people, all yours: nobody else aboard.
  • No fixed timetable: you pick 2, 4 or 8 hours and the departure time.
  • Route to suit: decided with the skipper according to the wind and to what you feel like doing.
  • Anchoring and swimming wherever the water is best that day, with paddle surf and snorkelling gear aboard.

Fuengirola to Puerto Banús: about thirty minutes

It is around 40 km. On the AP-7 that is a good 30 minutes; on the coastal A-7, considerably more in summer, because it threads through every town on the coast. Parking is inside Puerto Banús, right by the pontoon.

How does departure work?

Home port: Puerto Banús. Departure from Fuengirola available on request. The boat is berthed in Puerto Banús and that is where the charter starts by default. If you would rather board in Fuengirola, write to us with your date and we will look into it: it is something we assess case by case, not something we can take for granted in advance.

In practice the normal thing is to drive to Puerto Banús: half an hour and the boat is waiting. And there is a sailing reason for it, not just a logistical one — it is the next section.

The easterly rules this stretch of coast

Fuengirola sits on the eastern side of the bay, and that is the side that takes the levante, the easterly wind that blows here often. With a strong levante the sea between Fuengirola and Calahonda chops up and anchoring stops being comfortable.

Leaving from Puerto Banús that problem has an immediate fix: the skipper checks the forecast in the morning and, if the easterly is in, heads west — Guadalmina, El Saladillo, San Pedro — where the water stays sheltered and flat. From a port at the eastern end of the coast, that same move costs you half the charter in transit.

With a westerly it is the other way round and the good side is Cabopino and the Artola dunes. Which is why leaving from the middle of the coast is a genuine advantage: there is always a good side within reach.

The boat: one De Antonio D50 for ten people

There is no fleet to choose from: there is one boat, a 15-metre De Antonio D50 built in 2026 with 2× Mercury V12 600 hp. Which means that when you book, you already know exactly which boat you are getting, who is skippering it and what condition it is in — precisely what you do not know when you book through a listings platform.

It takes up to 10 people comfortably: swim platform aft, bow sunbed and an interior cabin. A professional skipper is included in every charter, so no passenger needs a boat licence.

Frequently asked questions

Do you depart from Fuengirola port?

Our home port is Puerto Banús. Departure from Fuengirola is available on request: send us your date and we will look at it for that day. It is not a standard departure we can confirm in advance.

How long is the drive from Fuengirola to Puerto Banús?

About 40 km and roughly 30 minutes on the AP-7. The coastal A-7 takes longer, especially in July and August.

How is this different from the boats leaving Fuengirola seafront?

Those are shared departures: individual seats, a fixed timetable and a set route. Here you book the whole boat for your group of up to 10, you choose the duration, and the route is decided between you and the skipper as the day goes.

What happens when the easterly blows?

The levante chops up the eastern side of the bay. Leaving from Puerto Banús the skipper can head west — Guadalmina, El Saladillo — and find sheltered water without spending half the charter getting there. That is the advantage of a central home port.

Shall we plan your day on the water?

We advise you with no obligation and confirm in under 2 hours.
