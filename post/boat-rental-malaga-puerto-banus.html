Boat rental from Málaga: the full-day escape to Marbella

Updated August 2026 · 7 min read

Málaga has a port, a city beach and open sea in front of it. What it does not have ten minutes away are the coves: the good anchoring coast — clean water, sand bottom, few people — starts west of the city and concentrates between Cabopino and Estepona. Which is why searching for boat rental in Málaga almost always turns into a logistics decision: how much driving is it worth?

Our answer is specific: it is worth it if the plan is a full day. Our home port is Puerto Banús, about 65 km away.

Málaga to Puerto Banús: 45 minutes to an hour

From central Málaga to Puerto Banús is about 65 km. On the A-7 (Autovía del Mediterráneo) or the AP-7 toll motorway, that is between 45 minutes and an hour depending on traffic. From the airport it is a little less — around 45 minutes — because you are already pointing west.

The AP-7 is the sensible choice in July and August: the coastal A-7 clogs up and the drive can stretch out considerably. Parking is inside Puerto Banús, right by the pontoon.

If you are arriving by plane or by AVE

This is the most common case. Málaga-Costa del Sol airport and María Zambrano station are both on the west side of the city, which is the useful side: you do not have to cross Málaga to head for Marbella.

  • Taxi or private transfer straight to the marina: simplest if there are only a few of you and you are down for the day.
  • Hire car: worth it if you are staying several days or if you are a large group. Parking inside Puerto Banús.
  • Train plus car: the Málaga cercanías line only runs as far as Fuengirola, so you still have to continue by road. It saves no time.

Whichever you choose, budget around 45 to 50 minutes from leaving the airport or the station to standing on the pontoon.

How does departure work?

Home port: Puerto Banús. Departure from Málaga available on request. The boat is berthed in Puerto Banús and that is where the charter starts by default. If you would rather board in Málaga, write to us with your date and we will look into it: it is something we assess case by case, not something we can take for granted in advance.

There is also another way to read the drive, and it works well for a lot of people: make it part of the plan. Come down in the morning, spend the whole day at sea and stay for dinner in Puerto Banús or Marbella before heading back. It stops being a transfer and becomes a full day out.

Why the full day is the plan that fits from Málaga

It is simple arithmetic of the day. Forty-five minutes in the car weighs heavily on a 2-hour charter and barely at all on an 8-hour one. But there is a sailing reason too: the full day is the only duration that genuinely reaches the ends of the coast with time to anchor, eat and swim without watching the clock.

A typical full day out of Banús: east along the Golden Mile and Río Real, first anchorage and swim towards Cabopino and the Artola dunes, lunch aboard, a second anchorage in the afternoon and back in the best light of the day. Or the same plan westwards, with Guadalmina and the Estepona coast. The skipper decides in the morning based on the wind.

And if you want the properly long run, the full day also covers the boat trip to Gibraltar.

Frequently asked questions

Do you depart from Málaga port?

Our home port is Puerto Banús, in Marbella. Departure from Málaga is available on request: send us your date and we will look at it for that day. It is not a standard departure we can treat as confirmed in advance.

How long is the drive from Málaga to Puerto Banús?

About 65 km from central Málaga: between 45 minutes and an hour depending on traffic and which road you take. From the airport it is less, around 45 minutes.

I am flying in or arriving by AVE — how do I get there?

Málaga airport and María Zambrano station are both on the western side of the city, on the same exit for the westbound motorway. From either one, a taxi or hire car puts you in Puerto Banús in about 45 to 50 minutes, with parking inside the marina.

Why do you recommend the full day from Málaga?

Because the drive weighs less the longer the charter is. Against 8 hours at sea, forty-five minutes in the car is a small part of the day; against a 2-hour charter it is not. The full day is also the only duration that genuinely reaches the ends of the coast with time to anchor.

Shall we plan your day on the water?

We advise you with no obligation and confirm in under 2 hours.
